Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi

Mahatma Gandhi was one of the greatest leaders in world history. Through truth, non-violence, and civil disobedience, he inspired millions of people to fight for justice and freedom without using violence.

About Gandhi

Mohandas Karamchand Gandhi was born on 2 October 1869 in Porbandar, Gujarat. He studied law in London and later worked in South Africa, where he experienced racial discrimination. These experiences shaped his philosophy of fighting injustice through peaceful resistance.


After returning to India in 1915, Gandhi became the leader of the Indian independence movement. His principles of Satya (Truth) and Ahimsa (Non-Violence) united millions of Indians against British rule.

Major Achievements

1893

Started fighting against racial discrimination in South Africa.

1915

Returned to India and joined the freedom movement.

1920

Launched the Non-Cooperation Movement.

1930

Led the historic Dandi Salt March against British salt laws.

1942

Started the Quit India Movement demanding complete independence.

1947

Witnessed India's Independence after decades of struggle.
